Engineering Formulas

Annular capacity (bbl/ft)

(Hole diameter² − Pipe OD²) / 1029.4

Annular capacity (bbl/m)

Capacity (bbl/ft) × 3.28084

Annular velocity (ft/min)

24.51 × Flow Rate (GPM) / (Hole² − Pipe OD²)

Hydrostatic pressure (PSI)

0.052 × Mud Weight (PPG) × TVD (ft)

ECD (PPG)

MW + Annular Pressure Loss / (0.052 × TVD in ft)

These simplified equations do not calculate full SPP, torque/drag, surge/swab, kick tolerance or cuttings transport.
